Add `_cmp.tabs` — 18 component tokens for Tabs. The component has existed in Figma since `v0.1.0` but its tokens were never synced to the repo.

Covers `tab-menu-item` in `active`, `inactive` and `disabled` states — each with `surface`, `indicator`, `label` and `icon` — plus hover and pressed surfaces and item padding.
