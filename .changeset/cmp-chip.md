Add `_cmp.chip` — 34 component tokens for Chip. The component has existed in Figma since `v0.1.0` but its tokens were never synced to the repo.

- **`filled`**: `surface-*` / `text-*` for `info`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`, plus neutral `light` and `dark`.
- **`subtle`**: the same, plus `border-*` per type.
- **Structure**: `padding/{left-right,top-bottom}`, `radius/default` (pill), `border/width`.
