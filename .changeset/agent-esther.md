Add Esther, the accessibility-review persona, to the `cornerstone-dev` plugin — one of the five agents specced on the persona map (#60), scoped in #65 and amended by #67, #74 and #75.

She is the WCAG 2.2 authority over Cornerstone's tokens, components and patterns, and a blocking review on every component PR. No `model:` key: #74 puts her on the session's inherited model rather than an Opus floor, since her work today is manual review against documented convention.

Two amendments changed what she is. #75 retired `design-review`, so she has nothing deterministic to invoke and performs the WCAG checks herself until UIUX-96 ships — the file says so and tells her not to run the deprecated command. #67 found matthewlarn's 33 accessibility skills to be `.skill` ZIP archives in a repo with no LICENSE, so the ten capabilities #65 adopted (`contrast-checker`, `keyboard-focus-auditor`, `motion-auditor` and the rest, plus `design-system-drift` narrowed to accessibility drift) are named as skills she will author against WCAG 2.2 directly, explicitly flagged as not existing yet.

Written against the repo rather than the spec, so several claims land sharper than #65 stated them. `npm run validate` enforces E1–E4 aliasing rules and knows nothing about perception — it passes an indicator at 1.46:1 because every alias in the chain is legal — and `ci.yml` runs three jobs, none of them accessibility, with no axe or test runner in `devDependencies`. The `_cmp.tabs` failure is carried in full with the ratios recomputed per mode (1.46:1 in `cru-light`, already 12.84:1 in `cru-dark`, 3.15:1 in `fl-dark`), because it shows that one `_sys` name is four checks, that a 3:1 indicator fix is not a 4.5:1 text fix, and that `primary-strong` still exists only on an unmerged branch. `_sys.color.text.*` alias 8-digit `_ref` primitives, so contrast there requires alpha compositing before comparison — and `text.disabled` at 2.85:1 is exempt under 1.4.3, not a finding. Both shipped adapters embed resolved hexes rather than `var(--…)`, and the MUI adapter overrides `contrastText` from the `on-*` tokens, so a token fix reaches those consumers only when a human regenerates them and MUI's own contrast fallback is off.

Her CI guidance is written against the existing workflow's own hard-won lessons: scope `pull-requests: write` to her job because job-level `permissions` replaces the workflow-level block, keep a blocking job free of a job-level `if:` because a skipped job reports success to a required check, and pass PR-derived values through `env:` rather than into a shell. And `.github/CODEOWNERS` already opens with a `* @rguinee` catch-all, so #65's "no CODEOWNERS on her workflows" takes a deliberate entry rather than an omission — flagged for a human instead of edited.
