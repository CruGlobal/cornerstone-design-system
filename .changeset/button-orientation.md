Added: `cs-button` takes `orientation="vertical"` to stack its `start` slot above the label and `end` below it, spaced by `--cs-button-stacked-gap`, `--cs-button-stacked-padding-block` and `--cs-button-stacked-padding-inline`. It reads `--cs-button-justify-content` to align a one-row button's content. Buttons in a horizontal `cs-button-group` share one height. The Button page gains Stacked, Navigation Bar and Content Alignment examples.
